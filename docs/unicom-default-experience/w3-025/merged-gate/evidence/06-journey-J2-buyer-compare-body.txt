How to read this surface — three kinds of truth, never mixed

Prices (verified, stale) — authoritative: Computed by the deterministic commerce runtime from committed demo price lists. Exact money, not estimates; a verified price is what it says it is.

Stock, delivery, protection — provider observations: Reported by seller feeds with source timestamps and ages: verified, stale, UNKNOWN or unavailable. They describe what the feed said, never what the kernel knows.

Buy-now-vs-wait guidance — model prediction: Produced by the model, always labelled predictive and never a promise. It lives on the Intent canvas and the Decide surface — never inside a price.

Freshness legend (UNKNOWN ≠ FAILED)

verified: Confirmed by the seller's feed within the last 30 minutes (demo clock).

stale: Confirmed once, but older — the price stays visible with its age and a caution.

UNKNOWN: The feed could not confirm. No price, no stock, no promise — and not an error.

unavailable: The seller or feed says the offer is off the table. Honest, not hidden.
