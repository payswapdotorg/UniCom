Start with what you need
buyer path

The Buyer Intent Canvas turns your own words into a working brief. Type the need here — the draft is kept while you switch roles — then open the canvas for the full 17-facet constraint editor, plan options and buy-or-wait guidance.

What do you need? (your own words — kept while you switch roles)

The essentials the canvas understands (all optional — leave anything open):

Needed by (deadline) — The last date this still works for you. Plans are checked against it before anything else.
Total budget (max-total-cost) — The all-in ceiling — price, delivery and fees together — not a starting point to negotiate up.
Quality (min-quality) — The floor you can accept: any, solid or premium. Options below your floor never reach you.
Trust & privacy (seller trust, privacy) — How much you need to trust the seller (verified, top-rated) and what they may do with your data.
Delivery or pickup (delivery-pickup-constraints) — How it must reach you: delivery, pickup, or either — timing you cannot accept is filtered out.
Alternatives (acceptable-substitutes) — What you would happily take instead. Substitutes widen your options; you decide what counts.

What you can rely on — three kinds of truth, never mixed
truth class
Authoritative operational state

What the system knows is true now — orders, stock and money as deterministic state. Exact, auditable, and never silently changed by a model.

truth class
Provider & physical observations

What sellers, feeds and physical counts report, always with source timestamps: verified, stale, UNKNOWN or unavailable. Observations never auto-promote to truth.

truth class
Model predictions

What the twin expects may happen — buy-or-wait guidance and what-ifs. Always labelled predictive; they become truth only through execution with evidence.
