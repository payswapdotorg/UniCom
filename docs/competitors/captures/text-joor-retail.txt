JOOR is a leading B2B wholesale platform that connects retailers with over 14,000 fashion brands worldwide. It also helps retailers with streamlining orders, inventory management, and wholesale payments. How can retailers discover new brands on JOOR? Retailers can browse curated linesheets, virtual showrooms, and collections, filtering by category, season, price, and trend to quickly find the perfect brands for their store. Can retailers place orders online through JOOR? Yes. Retailers can place and manage orders directly on JOOR, making wholesale buying faster, more accurate, and easier to track than email or PDF methods. Does JOOR work for both small boutiques and large chains? Absolutely. JOOR supports retailers of all sizes, from single-location boutiques to multi-store chains, helping scale buying efficiently across all locations. How does JOOR make reordering easier? View your previous orders and product quantities on JOOR to reorder quickly from the same brands while staying informed on new collections and seasonal releases. Can retailers manage multiple brands and seasons in one place? Yes. JOOR allows you to shop linesheets and collections across multiple brands and seasons, centralizing your orders in a single platform and giving you full visibility and control of your wholesale. Can my retail store source brands and place orders on JOOR if we already use another wholesale platform? Absolutely. JOOR is an open, flexible platform that coexists perfectly with other buying methods. Retailers frequently use JOOR to browse catalogs and place orders with our vast network of leading global brands, while at times using different platforms or direct EDI connections for other vendors. How does JOOR improve wholesale efficiency for retailers? With digital linesheets, order tracking, and analytics, retailers save time, reduce errors, and make data-driven buying decisions, all within one platform. Can JOOR integrate with Shopify? Yes, the JOOR Product Sync App for Shopify simplifies your wholesale-to-retail workflow by automatically syncing product data from your orders on JOOR to your Shopify account. You’re able to sync product data, including SKUs, images, descriptions and pricing, allowing you to get your products ready to sell much faster. How do retailers benefit from JOOR’s global network? Retailers gain access to curated brands from around the world, enabling them to diversify their assortment, discover trending products, and stay competitive. Do both boutiques and department stores use JOOR? Yes. Retail buyers of all sizes, from independent boutiques and specialty stores to multi-brand e-commerce sites and leading international department stores, use JOOR as their go-to wholesale buying platform. How do I get started with JOOR as a retailer? Open an account today to discover and buy from brands on JOOR. If you are a larger department store or multi-brand e-commerce website, you might be interested in our more advanced assortment planning platform, JOOR’s Pro Retailer solution, which is available for a fee.
