Now It Can Tell You. Strategic Sourcing FAQs How does strategic sourcing work? The traditional strategic sourcing approach starts with identifying opportunities through spend analysis and classification. This is followed by sourcing categories that exceed certain predefined spend and volume thresholds (or both), as well as categories identified as having potential future importance. Then, negotiated value is codified by creating contracts that are linked to transactions. In ideal situations, these contracts would result in all the negotiated savings being “realized” as spend occurs. However, strategic sourcing is not immune to common procurement challenges such as rogue contracts or spend. That’s why it’s essential to combine strategic sourcing with strong visibility and effective management of transactional spend. Does strategic sourcing handle high-spend, low-dollar value categories in the same way? Large enterprises today have thousands of categories to source and procure. With the reality of limited resources, all companies prioritize sourcing activities. Automation of tail spend contracts, combined with routine sourcing events, addresses standardized low-dollar-value categories. Advanced sourcing events address high-spend and critical categories. However, this approach results in a large set of categories being ignored or sourced too infrequently, which likely leaves money on the table. These missed categories represent a lucrative opportunity to capture additional value. This large set of spend categories can be optimized with externally managed community sourcing events, which are professionally run events that aggregate spend from multiple organizations. This ensures no category is left unmanaged, maximizing total company savings. Who uses strategic sourcing software? anchorStrategic sourcing software is used by procurement, sourcing, finance, and supply chain teams at organizations of all sizes. While it’s most common in mid-sized and enterprise companies, small and start-up firms — especially those sourcing large volumes of direct materials — also use it to manage suppliers, control costs, and make more informed purchasing decisions. What type of sourcing events can I run on the platform (RFP, RFI, RFQ, Reverse Auctions)? With Coupa, you can run a wide range of sourcing events to meet your procurement needs, including: RFx events , such as Request for Information (RFI) , Request for Quote (RFQ) , and Request for Proposal (RFP) , to gather information, pricing, and proposals from suppliers. RFx events can also be combined with an auction in the pre-bidding phase. Reverse auctions , where suppliers competitively bid to lower prices, such as English reverse auctions , Dutch reverse auctions , and Japanese reverse auctions . These event types support both simple bidding and complex strategic sourcing scenarios. What does community sourcing do that strategic sourcing might not? Using strategic sourcing software, community sourcing connects businesses with common purchasing needs, enabling them to collaborate and leverage the group’s combined spend volume and sourcing expertise for group events. There are three distinct benefits of community sourcing: capturing savings, reducing workloads, and optimizing quality and terms. The value of community sourcing for procurement professionals also includes forming new peer connections and gaining access to expertise beyond the organization. Many sourcing veterans find that collaborating through community sourcing programs leads to new insights and inspiration. How does sourcing optimization help with complex categories like logistics and packaging? Sourcing optimization helps manage complex categories, such as logistics and packaging, by evaluating multiple variables simultaneously — including lanes, volumes, service levels, constraints, and supplier capacity — to sharpen the overall process. Instead of comparing bids line by line, it analyzes scenarios to identify the best combination that balances cost, performance, and risk, helping teams make smarter sourcing decisions. For packaging, for instance, it enables teams to compare options across materials and formats while factoring in sustainability and ESG criteria such as recycled content and supplier certifications. Does the software provide an audit trail for sourcing activities and decisions? Yes, Coupa’s strategic sourcing software provides a complete audit trail for sourcing activities, capturing everything from setup to supplier communications, bid submissions, evaluations, approvals, and award decisions. This creates clear documentation for compliance, governance, and internal and external audits. How does the software help reduce cycle time for RFPs? Coupa’s strategic sourcing software reduces RFP cycle times through automation and pre-saved sourcing content. Teams can duplicate past events with templates, questions, pricing structures, and supplier lists — all automatically pre-filled, eliminating the need for manual setup and rework. Built-in workflows, standardized approval chains, supplier self-service, and real-time collaboration further accelerate communication and decision-making. Together, these capabilities help teams launch RFPs faster, manage bids more efficiently, and reach award decisions sooner. Want to see Coupa in action?
