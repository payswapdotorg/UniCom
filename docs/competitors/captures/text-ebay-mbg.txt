Whatever you’re buying—large or small, new or refurbished—eBay offers many ways to ensure you get exactly what you want. eBay Money Back Guarantee Get the item you ordered or your money back—it’s that simple. It’s automatic and covers your purchase price plus original shipping on eligible purchases* and there’s no extra fee for coverage. Follow these steps to get your refund.
