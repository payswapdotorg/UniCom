What features help prevent maverick or off-contract spend? Coupa’s procurement software stops rogue spending through a combination of:  AI-powered guided buying ensures users choose contract-approved goods. A unified procure-to-pay process enforces policy and compliance at every step.  Centralized contract management automatically surfaces preferred items and validates invoices.  Real-time spend visibility and analysis flag non-compliant or outlier purchases.  Access to the Coupa Advantage Marketplace offers instant, discounted contract-based shopping. SpendGuard™ uses AI to monitor suspicious behavior across users and suppliers to spot risk and stop fraud.   These capabilities make it easy for users to buy compliantly and within company policy while giving procurement teams the tools to enforce adherence, drive savings, and close compliance gaps. How does Coupa procurement software support supplier onboarding and management? Coupa makes it easy to add suppliers to the platform and manage them throughout the relationship lifecycle. Coupa also provides tools to monitor risk, compliance, and diversity credentials, ensuring that you work with the right suppliers while meeting internal and external requirements.
