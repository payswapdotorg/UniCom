Here are the top questions How does MarketMan automate restaurant purchasing? + MarketMan monitors your inventory levels against par levels you set for each item. When stock drops below the par threshold, the system automatically generates a suggested purchase order for the relevant supplier. You review and approve the order with one click, then it's sent directly to the supplier. Can I manage orders from multiple suppliers in one place? + Yes. MarketMan consolidates all your supplier relationships in one platform. You can manage catalogs, pricing, and order history for every supplier, compare prices across vendors, and send orders to multiple suppliers simultaneously during your daily ordering routine. How does digital receiving work in MarketMan? + When a delivery arrives, your team opens the purchase order in the MarketMan mobile app and checks off items as they're received. Any quantity discrepancies or missing items are flagged immediately. The system updates your inventory counts automatically once the delivery is confirmed. What POS systems does MarketMan integrate with for ordering? + MarketMan integrates with all major POS platforms including Square, Toast, Lightspeed, and others. Sales data from the POS is used to calculate theoretical inventory usage, which feeds directly into the automated reorder calculations to keep ordering precise. Can MarketMan help me reduce over-ordering? + Yes. By using sales-based par level calculations and real-time inventory visibility, MarketMan ensures you order only what you need. Spend analytics reports show ordering patterns over time, helping you identify seasonal trends and optimize order quantities to reduce waste from over-purchasing.
