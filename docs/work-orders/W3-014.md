# W3-014 — Commerce Incumbent Evidence and Human-Validation Readiness

Phase: Post-V3 Real-World Validation  
Lane: Worker 3 — Industry Evidence / Competitor Method / Research Readiness  
Status: READY  
Authority: docs/development-state/post-v3-validation-state.json  
Architecture baseline: 1.1-frozen-2026-10-05

## Objective

Produce fair, inspectable evidence for how UNiCOM performs against the commerce tools firms actually use for comparable in-scope workflows, and prepare a responsible protocol for validating adoption assumptions with real professionals.

UNiCOM is commerce-only. Do not score it overall against broad non-commerce platforms such as Autodesk, Procore, clinical/EHR systems, fleet-dispatch systems, creative-production suites or legal matter-management software.

## Scope A — incumbent comparison

1. Use docs/simulations/V3-INDUSTRY-AND-COMPETITOR-MATRIX.md to select industry- and workflow-appropriate commerce tools: procurement/supplier portals, marketplaces, POS/inventory/replenishment systems, storefront/order systems, rental marketplaces and B2B channels.
2. Freeze an equivalent task pack before comparison. Compare only matched commerce capabilities: sourcing, offer comparison, buyer intent, group buys where supported, rental/borrow, resale/consignment, multi-hop trades where supported, procurement/receiving, orders/fulfilment/returns, local-edge commerce and applicable safety/recourse behavior.
3. Assign every claim one evidence class: A = authorized actual account/UI/sandbox; B = authentic official interactive demo/public UI; C = official documentation checklist only; D = unverified/inaccessible. Class D cannot support performance superiority claims.
4. Capture the exact vendor/product/edition, date, access mode, URL, observed workflow, task preconditions, evidence pointers and limitations. Record timing only when measured with a reproducible method under comparable conditions. Do not invent UI actions, features, prices, latency or market position.
5. Separate “not offered,” “not verified,” “not accessible,” and “not applicable.” A tool being unavailable to the evaluator is not proof that the competitor lacks a feature.
6. Publish workflow-level comparison tables rather than an unjustified single aggregate winner. Retain negative findings for UNiCOM and incumbents.

## Scope B — real-human validation readiness

Prepare a reviewable study package with recruitment criteria, role/industry/firm-size sampling plan, task scripts, consent language, privacy/data-retention approach, interview/usability questions, success and failure measures, bias/limitations register, withdrawal process and a plan to report denominators and missing data. Keep real human evidence separate from synthetic persona outputs.

Do not recruit, contact, record or analyze identifiable participants until the operator has explicitly authorized the study and the required informed-consent, privacy and institutional/legal approvals have been resolved. Do not place participant personal data, credentials or recordings in the repository.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every incumbent claim points to captured evidence and an explicit A/B/C/D class.
- Comparisons are commerce-function-specific and use the same or explicitly mapped task definitions.
- Inaccessible and unsupported functions are marked unverified/not applicable, never fabricated.
- Any claimed UNiCOM advantage is supported by reproducible observations and disclosed limits; no market-wide or universal superiority claim is inferred from a few tasks.
- The human-validation package is ready for operator review but performs no unauthorized recruitment or participant research.
- Synthetic simulation estimates remain labelled synthetic and are not blended with human observations.

## Required deliverables

- Versioned incumbent evidence ledger and workflow-level comparison report.
- An evidence gap register grouped by industry, capability and evidence class.
- Human-validation protocol and consent/research materials for operator review.
- Explicit list of tasks blocked by account access, paid plans, permission or unavailable official demos.
