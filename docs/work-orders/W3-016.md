## Objective

Two merchant-side wirings to real runtimes, through permitted public
entrypoints only:

- **J5 merchant side** (`packages/web/src/commerce-modules/merchant-groupbuy-review/`):
  the latent-demand proposal review desk currently keeps accept/counter/
  reject in local demo state with a "not the kernel journal" label. Wire it
  to the W1-012 formation aggregate: merchant accept/counter/reject submit
  real kernel commands; threshold MONITORING, HELD pools and
  FORMED-with-final-terms render from kernel truth; the participant roster
  and consent terms render from the aggregate; the "no binding effect" and
  "not the kernel journal" statements are superseded by the honest new state
  (kernel-journaled facts; the merchant decision desk remains a review
  surface, not an override of buyer consent).
- **J15 connector manager** (`packages/web/src/commerce-modules/merchant-connectors/`):
  the integration manager currently renders committed fixtures
  ("connected=none today, honestly"). Wire it to the REAL connector runtime
  that already exists in the experience plane — `@unicom/experience`
  (contract surfaces: connector transports, health-surface, observability,
  live-commerce, feed-file, browser-session, connector-studio) and, where
  browser-compatible, `@unicom/experience/runtime`. Surface real connector
  lifecycle/health/journey outcomes; exercise at least one provider adapter
  **safely** (its typed non-live surfaces — health observation, capability
  declaration, journey evidence — per the runtime's own execution-mode
  permission matrix); no credentials, no live provider calls, no purchases/
  payments/orders anywhere.
