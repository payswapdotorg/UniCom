# W3-012 — Cycle-1 Remediation Order 2: Persona Journey Coverage + W2→W1 Vocabulary Mapping + Amended Baseline Re-Run 2

Lane: Worker 3 — Experience / Runner / Campaign execution
Status: READY (dispatched 2026-10-09T11:20:00Z)

Phase: cycles.improvement_1 (measurement repair, order 2)

## Objective
Repair the second harness-class measurement gap isolated by the TL in the
amended baseline analysis: **persona journey coverage**. The campaign
currently executes every project's journeys as `personaIds[0]` with the
hardcoded role `"project-owner"` — only 39 of 15,275 personas (one per firm)
ever accumulate evidence; the other 15,236 personas score ~4-5 with empty
bundles (every mapper component zeroes on `records.length === 0`), which is
why the amended outputs show exactly 39 eligibles. Additionally, the W2
persona `applicableJourneys` vocabulary (persona-journeys.ts: "compare-
sellers", "buy-vs-wait-negotiate", "existing-groupbuy-discovery", ...) has
never been reconciled with W1's authoritative journey-family ids
("offer-sourcing-comparison", "buy-now-vs-wait-price-timing" +
"negotiation-substitution", "existing-group-buy", ...) — a third vocabulary
seam of the same class the W3-011 register documented.

The product is unchanged (all 48,300 journeys pass). This is measurement
repair: the adoption outputs must measure ALL 15,275 personas over their
applicable journeys.

## TL quantitative analysis (the evidence for this order)

- A fully-evidenced persona (all-pass bundle, generic-driver evidence):
  weighted score ≈ 82 (crosses both thresholds 65/55 → eligible + willing).
- An empty-bundle persona: score ≈ 4 (matches the observed global mean 5).
- The product ceiling with perfect evidence (healthy connectors, P5 proof):
  ≈ 95.
- Therefore the 39/15,275 outputs are an artifact of single-persona
  attribution, NOT a product measurement.

## Scope
1. **W2→W1 journey vocabulary mapping**: a deterministic, test-pinned
   mapping from the W2 persona `applicableJourneys` ids to W1's
   authoritative journey-family ids. Every W2 id must map to ≥1 W1 family
   (or be recorded as unmappable with the reason — do NOT silently drop).
   The mapping lives in the campaign/mapper surface (experience package);
   the W2 artifact itself is READ-ONLY (frozen).
2. **Persona bundle attribution**: replace the `personaIds[0]` execution
   with per-persona (or per-role-family, attributed to personas) journey
   coverage: every persona's bundle must contain evidence for the W1
   families their applicableJourneys map to. Acceptable designs (worker's
   choice, documented): (a) execute per role-family (record carries the
   role) and attribute to personas of that role; (b) execute per persona.
   Bounded run count with the reconciliation law holding. The record's
   role field must be a real role from the persona's roleFamily (no
   hardcoded "project-owner").
3. **Coverage law (test-pinned)**: every persona's bundle is non-empty AND
   covers ≥1 mapped applicable family; personas with zero applicable
   mapped families must be explicitly counted and disclosed (expected 0).
4. **Amended baseline re-run 2**: `--amendment=2` over the baseline
   namespace (3,900 projects; holdout untouched — §7). Both reconciliation
   laws hold; the four adoption outputs recomputed under the frozen
   w2-009:v1 contract (byte-identical contract file); the amendment record
   versioned alongside amendments 0 and 1 (before/after vs BOTH).
5. **Amendment record**: what changed, why (this order), the
   before/amendment-1/amendment-2 journey-family + persona-coverage
   tables, and the honest classification (measurement repair, not product
   improvement).
6. **Determinism**: byte-identical re-run modulo the isolated throughput
   block; fingerprint test.
7. **Cycle-1 product-ranking input**: the amended-2 report's §7-cycle-1
   readiness must now carry the REAL product findings (if any) — the
   per-role/per-industry tails of the adoption distribution with full
   persona coverage — for the TL's ranking.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The vocabulary mapping is total (every W2 id mapped or explicitly
   recorded as unmappable with reason) and test-pinned.
2. Every one of the 15,275 personas has a non-empty evidence bundle
   covering their mapped applicable families (or is explicitly counted +
   disclosed as zero-coverage with reason).
3. Journey records carry real roles (no hardcoded project-owner); the
   role-realistic attribution is documented.
4. Amendment 2 artifacts versioned (baseline-report.amended-2.* +
   BASELINE-REPORT-AMENDED-2.md); amendments 0 and 1 preserved
   byte-identical.
5. Both reconciliation laws hold (drift 0) at the new run count; the
   planned count reflects the coverage design (disclosed in the report).
6. Four adoption outputs recomputed under the frozen contract; every
   willingness number carries the synthetic-estimate qualifier.
7. Determinism fingerprint test passes.
8. Full battery ≥ 1,576 with zero regressions; typecheck for touched
   projects; lint 0 errors; architecture 0 violations (max-file-lines 400).
9. Completion report with commands, outputs, file pointers, the coverage
   design decision, and the honest classification.
