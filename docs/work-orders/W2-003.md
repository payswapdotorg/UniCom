# W2-003 — Organization / Opportunity Lab

Lane: Worker 2
Status: READY
Dependencies: W2-002 (merged @ d83a38b); consumes W1-002 commerce kernel opaque opportunity references (merged)

## Objective
Build the Opportunity Engine and organization coordination plane on the W2-002 agent kernel: buyer-intent epistemics, GroupBuy discovery/formation, merchant demand-generated GroupBuys, bounded TradeCycle discovery, actor-as-capability representation, and privacy-aware coordination — all under Lab promotion gates.

## Scope
Under `packages/agent` (+ the ZCode kernel integration points from W2-002, module-system conforming): Opportunity Engine over Buyer CommerceIntent (opportunity epistemics: candidate generation, scoring, lifecycle states — intent remains Worker-2-owned, commerce facts arrive only as opaque references the way W1-002 threads them); GroupBuy discovery and formation (discovery matching, joining, commitment semantics, threshold formation, failure/dissolution paths); merchant demand-generated GroupBuy (demand signal aggregation into merchant-visible opportunities without leaking raw buyer intent); bounded TradeCycle discovery (hop-bounded per the W2-001 cycle contracts — termination proven, no unbounded chains); actor-as-capability representation (principals and delegates surface as capability holders in organization structures — one capability vocabulary, no second model); privacy-aware coordination (coordination reveals the minimum necessary: cross-principal inference resistance, no raw-intent broadcast, minimized disclosures at every coordination step); organization lifecycle under Lab promotion gates — formation logic is born in the Lab and only promoted to runtime through explicit, evidence-bearing promotion (Experiment/Evaluation records; no un-promoted organization logic executes in the runtime plane); deterministic formation/cycle outcomes given identical inputs (replay-stable decisions).

## Truth distinctions
Opportunity scores and match candidates are ESTIMATES, never commerce truth; formation outcomes are authoritative only after commitment lands; UNKNOWN intent fields stay UNKNOWN (no inference-based promotion to known).

## Acceptance scenarios
1. GroupBuy formation: threshold-met groups form exactly once; below-threshold groups dissolve deterministically; duplicate join attempts are idempotent.
2. Merchant demand-generated GroupBuy: aggregated demand produces the merchant-visible opportunity with zero raw buyer-intent fields crossing the boundary (asserted by test, not convention).
3. Bounded TradeCycle: discovery terminates within the hop bound on adversarial graph shapes (rings, cliques, chain floods); no cycle executes without authorization.
4. Privacy: an adversarial observer test reconstructs no more buyer-intent information than the coordination contract explicitly discloses (cross-principal inference blocked).
5. Actor-as-capability: an organization position grants exactly the capabilities it was assigned — no ambient authority, no capability forgery.
6. Lab promotion: un-promoted formation/coordination logic is unreachable from the runtime plane; a promotion with complete evidence activates it; promotion records are append-only.
7. Determinism: identical intent/capability inputs produce identical formation decisions on replay.

## Constraints
No canonical commerce state (Worker 1 lane); no provider adapters or browser/credential handling (Worker 3 lane); delegates stay ephemeral/bounded; BLOCK stays final (immune system); credentials/PII never enter model context; no production mocks; no scope beyond the agent lane.
