What is real here, and what is not

Real (kernel-driven): Formation. Interest, withdrawal, threshold MONITORING, HELD pools and the formation with final terms run through the commerce kernel's GroupBuy formation aggregate, imported from @unicom/commerce — every pool state and roster below is kernel truth.

Demo-only: The pools, the participant identities and the seeded shares. They arrive as committed demo fixtures (DEMO-labelled) and open through the kernel's own command path.

Wired (new this phase): Executed by the commerce kernel. Your interest travels as a RECORD_GROUP_BUY_INTEREST command carrying your consent terms; the kernel holds a threshold-met pool until the organizer's explicit FORM_GROUP_BUY freezes the final terms (exact group price, exact pool value) and the consenting-only roster. No group-buy commitment is ever created by this aggregate — a commitment would exist only on the commerce planes, after formation.
